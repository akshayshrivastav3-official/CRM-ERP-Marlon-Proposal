01

What CRM and ERP each do — and why they belong together

Most companies run customer data and operations data in separate systems. Meridian merges them so every team, report and AI agent reads the same record.

CRM — Customer Relationship Management

Answers: who are our customers, what do they want, and how do we win and keep them?

  • One trusted record and full history for every customer (Customer 360)
  • Leads, pipeline, deals, quotes and sales orders
  • Contracts, subscriptions, loyalty and engagement
  • Support cases, omnichannel service, field service and warranty

ERP — Enterprise Resource Planning

Answers: how do we buy, make, store, ship, pay, account for and staff all of it?

  • Finance, accounting, planning and financial close
  • Procurement, supply chain, warehouse and logistics
  • Product engineering, manufacturing, quality and assets
  • People (HCM), projects, safety & compliance, retail POS

The problem the merge solves

Data lives in silosCustomer, order, invoice and inventory records get copied across tools and quietly drift out of sync.
Teams work blindA sales rep can't see an order stuck in the warehouse; finance can't see why a customer is upset.
AI stays shallowAn assistant plugged into only CRM or only ERP can never give a complete, trustworthy answer.

05

One customer message, traced end to end

A customer emails about a late order. This is where CRM, ERP and AI meet — and where a human stays in control.

Step 1

Customer emails

"Where's my order?" arrives through Omnichannel Support.

Step 2 · AI

Copilot reads the record

It opens the unified customer record and creates a service case.

Step 3 · AI

Service Agent checks status

It looks at the sales order, warehouse picking and shipment — same data, live.

Step 4

Decision

Autonomy Controls decide whether the agent can act alone or needs a person.

Within authorityThe agent resolves it directly and sends the customer a real, accurate update.
Needs judgementIt is routed to a human agent with the full context — order, shipment, history — already attached.
Every step is written to the Immutable Audit Trail: who or what did it, when, and why.
06

Trust & control for AI agents

The more an agent can do, the more tightly it is governed. These controls apply to every agent, every time.

Identity & access control

Every person and every agent has a defined role and sees only what that role permits.

Approval gates

Anything above an agent's authority — a discount, a refund, a commitment — waits for a human.

Immutable audit trail

Every action, human or AI, is permanently logged with its reasoning attached.

Consent & privacy

Customer consent is tracked at its source and respected wherever the data travels.

Encryption & key management

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit to enterprise security standards.

AI explainability

Every AI decision can be traced back to the data and policy that produced it.

08

Glossary of terms

Short meanings of the abbreviations used in this guide.

CRM
Customer Relationship Management — systems for sales, service and customer data.
ERP
Enterprise Resource Planning — systems for finance, supply chain, manufacturing, HR.
CPQ
Configure, Price, Quote — building accurate quotes from products and price rules.
SLA
Service Level Agreement — the promised response or resolution time.
CSAT
Customer Satisfaction score, usually from a short survey.
GL
General Ledger — the main set of accounting books.
AP / AR
Accounts Payable (what you owe) / Accounts Receivable (what you're owed).
EPM
Enterprise Performance Management — planning, budgeting, forecasting and close.
BOM
Bill of Materials — the components needed to make a product.
CAPA
Corrective And Preventive Action — fixing a problem and stopping it recurring.
HCM
Human Capital Management — HR, payroll, recruiting and workforce.
EHS
Environment, Health & Safety.
POS / mPOS
Point of Sale / mobile Point of Sale for retail checkout.
EDI
Electronic Data Interchange — standard machine-to-machine business documents.
DLP
Data Loss Prevention — controls that stop sensitive data leaking.
